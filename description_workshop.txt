Function Summary
For a detailed list, please refer to the module configuration or update log
1. Items: The maximum stack of items is 1. Disable functions such as automatic swinging and the worst prefix
2. Player: Functions such as defense, health, magic power halving, and breathing time halving
3. Map: Remove dim light and other functions
4. NPC: Functions such as hiding NPC health bars and preventing NPCS from dropping items
Almost all functions are adjustable. You can choose the ones that suit you by yourself
